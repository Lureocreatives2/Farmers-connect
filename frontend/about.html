Our Mission

Closing the Gap Between Farm and Table

Nigeria has one of Africa's most productive agricultural sectors, yet farmers often earn a fraction of what their produce is worth — while buyers pay inflated prices through layers of middlemen.

Farmers Connect removes every barrier between producer and buyer. We give every Nigerian farmer a free digital storefront and connect them directly to buyers via WhatsApp — a tool every Nigerian already uses every day.

No commissions. No complicated apps. No gatekeepers. Just farmers and buyers doing business the way it should always have been done.

📅 Our Journey

How We Got Here

The Problem
Farmers earning pennies, buyers paying pounds
We watched Nigerian farmers struggle to reach buyers outside their local markets, while urban buyers paid 3–4× more through layers of middlemen. The gap was obvious — and fixable.
The Idea
A marketplace built for how Nigeria actually works
Instead of building yet another complex app nobody would use, we built around WhatsApp — the tool 90 million Nigerians already use daily. Simple listing forms, real photos, direct contact.
The Platform
Farmers Connect goes live
Farmers across all 36 states can now register, list their produce, and receive direct enquiries from buyers — all for free. No commission, no platform fees, no middlemen.
The Future
Building for scale
Next: farmer verification, in-app payments via Paystack, logistics integration, and SMS notifications via Termii so farmers in areas without smartphones can still participate.
