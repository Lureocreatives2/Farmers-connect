Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we get most often

🛒 For Buyers
How do I buy from a farmer?
+
Browse the Shop page, click any product to see full details, then click the green "Contact Farmer on WhatsApp" button. This opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message so you can discuss price and delivery directly with the farmer — no account needed.
Does Farmers Connect charge buyers any fees?
+
No — completely free for buyers. Farmers Connect charges zero commission and zero platform fees. You pay the farmer directly; we don't touch the transaction at all.
How do I know the produce is genuinely fresh?
+
Each listing includes a description from the farmer, which should mention harvest date and quality. We recommend asking directly via WhatsApp before committing to a large order. Always inspect goods on delivery before making payment. Verified farmer badges indicate farmers who have been manually confirmed by our admin team.
Can I search for produce in a specific state?
+
Yes — on the Shop page use the state dropdown filter to see listings only from farmers in a specific state. You can also combine it with category filters and search terms.
What if a listing looks fake or suspicious?
+
Use our Contact page and select "Report a Listing". Include the product name and reason. Our admin team reviews all reports within 24 hours and will remove any fraudulent or inappropriate listings.
🧑‍🌾 For Farmers
How do I register as a farmer?
+
Go to Register Your Farm and fill in your name, WhatsApp phone number, farm name, and state. You'll receive a unique Farmer ID — save it, you'll need it every time you list a product.
Is it free to list my products?
+
Yes — completely free, always. No listing fees, no sales commission, no hidden charges. You keep 100% of everything you earn from buyers.
How do buyers contact me?
+
Buyers click the WhatsApp button on your product listing. It opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message including your product name, so you know exactly what they're enquiring about — even before you read the message.
My listing is pending — when will it go live?
+
All new listings go through a quick admin review before being approved and made visible to buyers. This typically takes less than 24 hours. We check for accurate descriptions, appropriate pricing, and real product photos.
Can I list multiple products?
+
Yes — as many as you like. Visit List a Product for each product you want to add. Each gets its own dedicated page with photos, full details, and a direct WhatsApp contact button.
What makes a great listing?
+
Listings with photos get significantly more enquiries. The best listings include: a clear real photo of the actual product, harvest date and freshness details, minimum order quantity, packaging options, and whether you offer delivery. The more detail, the more trust you build with buyers.
⚙️ About the Platform
Does Farmers Connect handle payments or delivery?
+
Not at this stage. Farmers Connect connects buyers and farmers directly — payment and delivery are arranged privately between the two parties via WhatsApp. This keeps things simple and means no fees for anyone. Payment integration via Paystack is planned for a future release.
Which states does Farmers Connect cover?
+
All 36 states and the FCT. Any Nigerian farmer anywhere in the country can register and list their produce. We're actively working to grow farmer representation in under-served states.
What's a Verified Farmer badge?
+
Our admin team manually reviews and verifies certain farmers based on their activity, listing quality, and response rate. A Verified badge next to a farmer's name signals to buyers that this is a legitimate, active producer — helping build trust on the platform.
How do I report a problem or give feedback?
+
Use our Contact page. We read every single message and respond within 24 hours on weekdays. Your feedback directly shapes future features on the platform.
